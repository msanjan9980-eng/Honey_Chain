How it works

Four simple steps,
from hive to home.

Other systems rely on paperwork filled out after the fact. HoneyChain builds the record automatically, using real readings taken inside the hive itself.

1

Sensors watch the hive

Small, low-cost devices inside the beehive measure temperature, humidity, weight and sound — all day, every day.

Uses affordable hardware that works even in rural areas.
2

AI checks the bees

Smart software studies the readings to spot signs of illness, track the colony's health, and predict the next harvest.

Learns what a healthy hive looks and sounds like.
3

Records are locked in

When honey is harvested, a permanent digital record is created — one that cannot be edited or faked later.

Stored on a shared ledger that no single person controls.
4

You scan and see

Scan the QR code on the jar to see the beekeeper, the hive, the harvest date, and the health of the colony.

Opens in any phone browser. No app needed.
